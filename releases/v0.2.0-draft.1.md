The changelog and publication manifest identify every publication change while the original frozen code, requirements, proofs, and evidence retain their identities.

It reports the target/retention tradeoff, no-op outcomes under full-rank protection, nonzero realized residuals, native-precision effects, and repeated-edit limits.
