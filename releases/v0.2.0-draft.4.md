## How to interpret the evidence

The original freeze receipts, protocol identities, and recorded source hashes
are preserved. Historical study freeze, audit, and report checks remain
strict. The sanitized frozen lock and changed file inventory cannot pass
checks that require the original bytes. Saved passing receipts describe their
historical executions; the public sanitation ledger does not turn them into
a fresh passing replay of this snapshot.

The research conclusions are unchanged. The saved synthetic comparison shows
the documented target–retention tradeoff, and the historical RTS selection is
the unchanged baseline. No live-provider result, useful large-model editing
claim, new scientific study, or new Lean proof run is added. The
[evidence guide](../EVIDENCE.md) connects those conclusions to their inputs,
uncertainty, negative outcomes, and proof premises.

A new study needs clean inputs, a newly fixed protocol, and a fresh freeze and
output tree. Published seeds are already observed. The
[reproduction guide](../REPRODUCING.md) distinguishes package checks, saved-data
replay, reproduction, and confirmation on untouched units.
