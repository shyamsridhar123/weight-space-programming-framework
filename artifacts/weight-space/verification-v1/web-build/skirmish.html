A FIELD GUIDE

Command the Riverlands

GatherSelect villagers, then right click trees, berries, gold or stone. Villagers carry resources back to your town.

BuildOpen Build, pick a building, then click a clear explored site. A villager constructs it over time.

GrowSelect the Town Center to train villagers. Houses raise your population cap; farms provide more food.

ConquerRecruit at military buildings. Right click an enemy to attack, or use Attack move to advance while fighting.

Click or drag to select · Shift adds to selection · Right click issues an order · F sets attack move · X stops selected units · I selects idle villagers · M selects the army · WASD / arrow keys pan · Wheel zooms · H centers town · Space pauses · Esc cancels

Victory: destroy every rival Town Center. Spearmen counter knights; knights threaten archers; rams break buildings. Unexplored land and hidden enemies remain concealed.
