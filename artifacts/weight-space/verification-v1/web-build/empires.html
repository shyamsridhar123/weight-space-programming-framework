PLAY & PROGRAM

Watch the policy change.

  1. Let the AI play

    Choose a goal in AI + Astra and press Start AI + Astra. Blue’s neural policy issues real orders. The panel measures its progress and shows which performance target needs attention.

  2. Follow Astra’s weight edits

    When progress falls short, Astra receives observations and current policy weights. It generates numerical edits to the game policy. The panel shows the exact old and new values, the changed decision, and what happens afterward. This changes the game policy without a fine-tuning step.

  3. Stop or take command

    Stop Astra ends automatic weight reviews. Use Pause to hold the game. Take command opens manual play; issuing a manual blue order stops its automatic policy. Click blue units or use the selection shortcuts, then right-click resources to gather, open ground to move, or red enemies to attack.

  4. Build, recruit, conquer

    In manual play, select a villager → Build house → click open ground. Select Barracks → Train spearman. Train 8 more to join your two guards, then select Army → Attack enemy town. Destroy red’s Town Center and protect yours.

  5. Explore the evidence

    The paired comparison runs baseline and edited weights from the same game state. Model requests, history & scope records the provider evidence. Manual lab lets you edit one weight yourself; All weights exposes the full policy. The optional Laya chooser selects developer-authored presets.
