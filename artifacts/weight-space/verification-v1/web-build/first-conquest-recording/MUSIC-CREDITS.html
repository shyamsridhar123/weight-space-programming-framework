How the music is used

The film reuses the existing authorized soundtrack. The prepared bed is 90.000000 seconds, 48 kHz stereo PCM s16le, with its existing three-second entrance fade, five-second ending fade at 85–90 seconds, and volume envelope.

The assembler applies unity gain and encodes that bed to AAC, preserving the existing preparation without another attenuation or fade. Gameplay acceleration does not change the music’s speed. No earlier gameplay footage, experiment captions, or old video export is reused.

Earlier-export loudness figures in the source credit are historical. The final film has its own independent encoded-audio measurement summary; neither set of technical measurements constitutes a perceptual listening review.
