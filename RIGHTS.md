It is an author blog report, not a versioned arXiv or peer-reviewed paper. The [original actor conversion notice](docs/research/original-source-inputs-NOTICE.md), [repeat acquisition record](artifacts/research/original-source-repeat/acquisition.json), and [retained repeat-source license](artifacts/research/original-source-repeat/source/LICENSE) cover the adapted actor values.
