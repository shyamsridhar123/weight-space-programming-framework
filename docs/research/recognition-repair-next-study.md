# Prospective trained support-recognition repair study

**Draft date: November 9, 2026. Status: design proposal; execution is not authorized by this document.**

This proposal addresses the next scientific question under Issue #7: can a retained readout edit produce useful recognition repair on fresh requests, compared with a tuned replacement readout and ordinary policy or context methods, at an explicitly measured cost? It is a support-domain, single-change study. It does not add a recognition result, close a cost gap, establish cumulative editing, or claim a general-purpose weight reader.

The source review used confirmed main `76332efdb8a6b1e4b68644c9c8f779c5a4a5b4ac`. The saved-cohort verifier is referenced at PR #51 head `21bab1fc3d10b7d324d17b037de904f7f986e72f`; its engineering scope is described below. These are source identities, not new execution receipts. Historical scientific arrays and private manifests were not opened to prepare this draft. No project, validator, frozen runner, study, inference, or provider call was executed.

## 1. Why this study, and what would be learned

The existing [trained-applications report](trained-applications.md#recognition-uncertainty-and-the-attainable-gain-ceiling) reports no useful recognition success. Its published attainable-gain analysis shows that a perfect repair could not meet the historical ten-percentage-point target on several assigned cases. The optimistic all-case useful-coverage ceilings were `.625` for support and `.50` for digits, before any protected-harm or implementability constraint. Those results and historical margins remain unchanged.

The proposed study asks a narrower, answerable question: after selecting repair tasks using separate development evidence and freezing a feasible utility rule, how often does a method deliver a useful deployed change on all requests in a target intent? The strongest primary comparator is tuned head retraining. The historical ownership-only overlay does not repair intent recognition; this proposal adds an explicitly different predicted-confusion policy control that can do so.

Three conclusions must remain separate:

1. **Useful repair:** an arm improves the parent enough while respecting the protected criteria, with adequate all-assigned coverage.
2. **Superiority to a serious alternative:** soft retention improves target behavior more than tuned head retraining by the prespecified worthwhile margin. Useful repair alone does not establish this.
3. **Resource advantage:** a method uses fewer measured resources at comparable useful behavior and coverage. A fast rejected proposal or unchanged fallback does not establish this.

A negative or inconclusive result is a valid outcome. If an ordinary policy or context method meets the useful frontier more cheaply, the recommendation should favor it. A useful single-change result would justify considering a separately frozen cumulative study; it would not itself establish cumulative efficacy.

## 4. Tasks, pairing, and common access

The native decision rule is the lowest-index maximum intent score followed by the fixed project-derived queue map `Q`. A primary dispatch is correct exactly when `Q(predicted_intent) = Q(authored_true_intent)` under that frozen mapping. This is correctness under project policy, not validated bank operations. Report original-intent correctness separately, together with confidence/score behavior, target newly introduced errors, and per-intent results. An intent error that preserves the queue is not counted as primary dispatch harm.

For each potential target intent, calculate the parent’s cross-queue error rate on `D`, across the eight proposed pilot lineages. Before inspecting `D`, freeze a mean-error eligibility floor `eta_mean`, a per-lineage floor `eta_lineage`, and the required pilot count `k_eligible`. They must be justified by the absolute utility floor and the desired headroom, not selected to favor an arm; all three are currently unfilled. Select the six eligible intents with the largest mean error rate, breaking ties by the frozen intent ordering. If fewer than six qualify, stop this proposal at development; do not fill the roster from final mistakes. This selection favors difficult development tasks and limits generalization to this declared selection procedure and fixed case list.

For each selected case, freeze the two non-target predicted intent labels with the greatest cross-queue confusion counts on its `D` target rows; break ties by intent order. They are the confusion-neighbor set and define the ordinary policy configurations. If only one occurs, the second is absent and duplicate configurations collapse without creating extra trials. Selection of neighbors uses parent outputs only, not a preferred arm’s success.

For case `j`, target rows are **all** eligible requests whose true intent is `j`. Protected rows are all other intents, equally weighted by intent in the primary summaries. Final target rows are never filtered to parent mistakes. Each case begins from its unchanged full parent; edits are not composed. The six cases share parents and may share protected requests, so their rows are not independent experimental units.

Every method receives the same frozen parent, task description, queue map, legacy parent-training labels, `F` target/protected labels and features, and access entitlement to `C`. Actual information consumed is logged. Methods may ignore available information, but cannot acquire extra examples, model calls, test feedback, or labels. The methods’ loss functions differ intentionally. For example, head retraining uses labels while score retention can preserve an erroneous parent score; this difference must be visible.

An information/accounting manifest records, per arm, training ancestry; labels and raw text seen; feature/score/weight access; protected anchors; context-memory content; calibration feedback; candidate count; optimizer/checkpoint steps; example presentations; and all preparation. Do not call eight options equal compute or equal training effort.

## 5. Bounded comparator set and candidate selection

The eight arms below are the whole proposed main comparison. Each arm has at most eight candidate endpoints per lineage/case, one deterministic selected endpoint, and an unchanged-parent fallback. A no-op is recorded directly rather than padded into eight artificial candidates. Every observed checkpoint or threshold choice that can be selected counts as an option; concealed restarts and analyst-selected candidates are not allowed.

| Arm | Proposed candidate roster and implementation boundary |
| --- | --- |
| `unchanged` | One unchanged parent. It incurs shared serving and reporting cost and no invented proposal work. |
| `policy` | Remap a predicted confusion intent to the target intent only when the parent’s top-two softmax-score gap is at most a gate. Use the first one or both frozen confusion labels, crossed with gates `.05, .15, .30, 1.0`: at most eight candidates. No true label is available at inference. The gap is a defined numeric gate, not a claim of calibrated confidence. This is a new recognition-capable control, distinct from the historical ownership overlay. |
| `context` | Store the common `F` target and protected embeddings. Let `sT` and `sP` be maximum cosine similarity to a target and protected example. Override to the target only when `sT >= tau` and `sT - sP >= margin`; otherwise use the parent. Cross `tau = .50, .65, .80, .95` with `margin = 0, .10`: eight candidates. Define zero-vector similarity and tie behavior before freeze. Include memory construction, retrieval, storage, and raw-input encoding in cost. |
| `target_only` | Ridge readout displacement toward the true-intent one-hot target on `F` target rows, with penalties `.0001, .001, .01, .1, .3, 1, 3, 10`. No protected loss. |
| `soft` | The same mean target squared-score loss plus a mean squared protected-score-displacement penalty, with protected weight `.25, 1, 4, 16`, crossed with ridge `.001, .01`: eight candidates. Protected loss uses the frozen `F` protected subset described below. |
| `exact_score` | The same ridge target fit subject to zero score displacement on the frozen protected anchors. Cross numerical proposal cutoffs `1e-14, 1e-12, 1e-10, 1e-8` with ridge `.001, .01`: eight proposals. Numerical cutoff is not an exactness tolerance. Admission requires exact zero dyadic anchor residual for the stored endpoint, a separately checked native anchor-score equality condition, and the common behavioral conditions. |
| `decision` | Target cross-entropy plus a hinge penalty for violating each protected row’s original winning-intent logit ordering. Use a fixed `.01` winning margin, penalty `1, 8, 64, 512`, and `75` or `150` optimization steps: eight endpoints. Fixed Adam learning rate `.01`; ties and native realization are explicit. This is finite decision retention, not exact score retention. |
| `retrain` | Freeze the encoder and train a replacement readout initialized at the parent head on the available legacy training and `F` labels. Cross Adam learning rate `.005, .02`, target-intent oversampling `2, 8`, and `4, 12` epochs: eight endpoints, batch 256 and weight decay `.001`. Oversampling applies to target-intent examples in the combined fit pool. No calibration-based early stopping beyond these eight checkpoints. This is tuned head retraining, not full-model retraining. |

For displacement fits, the augmented feature row includes the bias coordinate; ridge penalizes every edited entry, including bias. Mean target and mean protected losses are normalized separately, with the ridge term `lambda * ||Delta W||_F^2`; no unreported sample-count or score rescaling changes these grids. Choose 128 protected fitting anchors by a frozen, intent-balanced rule from `F` and use the same anchors for soft, exact-score, and decision retention. All arms retain access to the rest of `F`; its actual use differs as stated. Keep anchor identities and any realized rank/residual diagnostic separate from behavioral outcomes.

If the row-oriented protected design has full **column** rank, its exact real score-preserving displacement is zero. Full row rank alone does not imply that conclusion. Exact score infeasibility does not rule out a useful decision change. Real-valued feasibility does not establish representability by the native endpoint. Interpret stored finite anchor features and endpoint entries as dyadic rationals for the structural residual check on `H_anchor * (W_new - W_parent)`. An exact zero there does not establish identical native floating-point matrix-product outputs, because evaluation order and rounding remain separate. This arm also requires exact native equality on those anchor scores under the frozen execution contract; report both checks and their distinct failure reasons. Neither finite-anchor condition establishes score equality on unseen requests. None of these diagnoses changes the assigned denominator or authorizes modifying a frozen historical path.

All fits share the same per-lineage seeds and deterministic seed derivation rule where appropriate; method-specific randomness is declared. Reuse of common computations or retraining trajectories is permitted only when disclosed and charged once at the shared boundary. Numeric time and memory limits must be set using the engineering pilot before freeze. On timeout, invalid realization, cancellation, or failed fit, retain the assigned option and cost, record the reason, and do not replace it.

On `C`, discard candidates that fail structural/native requirements or the proposed protected net, gross, or per-intent caps. Rank the remainder by target net gain, then smaller protected gross harm, smaller net harm, smaller displacement norm where applicable, and finally frozen roster index. Select at most one candidate. Do not use measured serving speed as an undisclosed tie breaker. If none qualifies, select unchanged fallback.

After the global main selection seal, assess each selected candidate once on `A` (pilot candidates use `V_A` instead). Require the same protected caps, the target utility rule, and the arm’s structural/native contract. A failed admission deploys the parent; it does not permit trying the next candidate. Preserve the selected-but-rejected proposal separately from the deployed endpoint. No admission pass is a final protected guarantee. Seal every main deployed endpoint, fallback status, contract, selection record, and ordered lineage/case/arm roster before opening `E`.

## 6. Utility, harm, zero-error cases, and missing outcomes

For nonempty target set `T_j`, let `e0` and `em` be the parent and deployed-method error fractions on **the same entire target set**. Define absolute net gain `g = e0 - em`. Let `R` count parent mistakes repaired and `B` count new target mistakes; equivalently, `g = (R - B) / |T_j|`. This credits repairs and charges collateral mistakes within the target class. If `T_j` is empty, both absolute rates and relative reduction are missing with reason `no_target_rows`; useful repair is false and the assignment remains. An empty evaluation set is not a zero-error parent.

When `e0 > 0`, report net parent-error reduction `r = g / e0`. It may be negative; do not clip it. Proposed useful gain requires both `g >= delta_abs` and `g >= rho * e0`, equivalently `g >= max(delta_abs, rho * e0)`, using the separately justified and frozen values. No numeric operational defaults are supplied. Use integer counts or declared exact comparisons at boundaries; rounded displayed percentages do not decide success.

The attainable gain is at most `e0`. Thus `e0 < delta_abs` makes this utility floor unattainable, even for perfect target predictions. When `e0 = 0`, relative reduction is undefined: report it as unavailable with reason `no_parent_target_errors`, retain absolute gain and the assigned unit, and set useful repair false. “No repair needed” is a separate outcome, not a successful intervention. The same applies to a correct unchanged parent. No evaluation-time task substitution or denominator removal is allowed.

For each protected intent `k`, define net harm `h_k = em_k - e0_k`; it may be negative. Its gross newly wrong rate `b_k` counts rows correct under the parent but wrong under the deployed candidate, divided by all evaluated rows of that intent. Primary protected summaries are the equal-intent means `h = mean_k(h_k)` and `b = mean_k(b_k)` across all 76 non-target intents; report row-weighted summaries separately. Also report repaired protected errors and decision flips, so netting cannot hide new failures. The harm gates are `h <= h_max`, `b <= b_max`, and every non-target intent’s net harm `h_k <= h_k_max`, with all caps justified and frozen. An empty required protected intent/stratum makes the relevant gate unavailable and therefore not passed; never omit it or renormalize onto surviving strata. Report gross harm and net harm for each frozen confusion neighbor as well. If a stricter neighbor or high-risk group cap is required, it must be defined and powered before freeze, not introduced after a bad subgroup result.

A lineage/case/arm useful indicator `u` is one only when a valid non-fallback endpoint satisfies target utility and every protected gate on `E`. A valid unchanged/no-op endpoint is never useful repair. The per-lineage useful fraction is `q_l = (sum_j u_lj) / 6`. All six assigned cases remain in that fraction.

| Outcome | Deployed-behavior accounting | Cost and analysis treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Valid selected/admitted endpoint | Evaluate the frozen native policy on all final target/protected rows | Retain its complete proposal and deployment cost |
| Rejection, abstention, no-op, or failed proposal with an intact evaluated parent | Parent fallback gives known zero gain and harm relative to itself; useful false | Retain fitting/selection/admission/failure cost; do not call failed proposal cost zero |
| Parent-training failure | No trained endpoint is known; this is not an unchanged successful parent | Keep assigned seed, useful false, cost and failure reason; no replacement lineage |
| Missing/corrupt target or protected evaluation | Gain/harm remain missing, not presumed zero | Useful false in operational coverage; retain worst-case sensitivity and prevent a positive confirmatory claim while unresolved |
| Interrupted or unmetered failure | Outcome and consumed resources may be unknown | Record last trusted boundary; no successful evidence or zero-cost imputation |

Report complete-pair estimates with their denominator alongside all-assigned coverage. For an unresolved failed parent/evaluation, give bounds over the valid outcome range rather than silently dropping it: each method gain lies in `[-1, 1]`, and the within-parent gain contrast between two valid classifiers lies in `[-1, 1]` because their parent term cancels. Use that tighter range only when the common-parent/target relationship is established; otherwise retain conservative missing-outcome bounds. A positive primary claim is blocked by unresolved primary evaluation integrity or by worst-case conclusions that change its sign or gate decision.

## 7. Independent units, uncertainty, multiplicity, and power

The training replication unit is a newly initialized and fully trained encoder plus head. Edited heads, checkpoints, cases, retries, copied exports, and serving repeats on a common parent are not additional independent lineages. With independently assigned training randomness, these are conditional algorithmic replications. They share the original corpus, train/development split, feature procedure, fitting/selection/admission pools, case-selection process, and final requests; they are not independent samples of data or task populations. The inference conditions on that shared ancestry and on isolated execution without outcome-driven shared adaptation. A broad population of learned readers, architectures, banks, task families, or future data shifts is not sampled here.

For the primary comparison, define `D_l = mean_j(g_soft,l,j - g_retrain,l,j)` over all six fixed cases. Use a paired two-sided 95% Student-t interval across the main full-lineage contrasts, conditional on the fixed final request set and common development choices. The worthwhile superiority gate is its lower endpoint greater than the separately justified and frozen `delta_comp`, not merely a positive point estimate. Report the two methods’ absolute gains, harm, rejection, and coverage next to the contrast. Beating a retraining arm that usually abstains does not remove the soft method’s own usefulness requirement.

For useful coverage, `q_l` is bounded in `[0, 1]`. Use the one-sided distribution-free lower bound `max(0, mean(q) - sqrt(log(1/.025) / (2 * N)))` across the full independently assigned lineage roster, treating missing useful outcomes as zero. Require this lower bound to exceed the justified frozen coverage target `c_min`. This intentionally conservative bound avoids treating six correlated cases as independent Bernoulli trials and does not collapse to certainty when every observed outcome is equal. Its conditional-independence assumptions and conservatism must remain explicit. Before planning power, check that even all-useful lineages could pass: `1 - sqrt(log(1/.025)/(2*N)) > c_min` is necessary. A different coverage procedure requires a new pre-evaluation freeze and justification; do not choose it because it passes.

Both useful coverage and worthwhile primary superiority must pass for the proposed positive primary conclusion. This is a conjunction: a failure of either blocks that conclusion. State the one-sided 2.5% error convention explicitly. The paired-t component is approximate; inspect prespecified distribution and influence diagnostics without selecting a more favorable test afterward. Degenerate empirical variance, too few valid full lineages, or severe dominance by a few lineages results in an estimation-only interpretation rather than a zero-width confirmatory interval. No inferential guarantee of whole-domain protected behavior follows from this analysis.

Run a prespecified crossed sensitivity analysis that resamples full lineages and common final author/scenario connected groups, preserving the same sampled group across every arm, parent, target/protected membership, and overlapping case. Recompute case denominators with the frozen intent weighting; do not independently resample each arm’s rows. Record and report resamples with empty required strata instead of dropping them invisibly. Report this request-population sensitivity separately from conditional lineage uncertainty. If it materially disagrees with the conditional analysis, limit the claim to the fixed evaluated request set. A beyond-case claim would require separately sampled task families and another protocol; these six cases do not supply that sampling.

There is one primary soft-versus-retraining contrast with the joint usefulness gate. Every other arm contrast, per-case result, original-intent metric, neighbor analysis, timing comparison, and historical stress result is descriptive. Do not promote the best arm after evaluation to a confirmatory winner. If a later revision needs multiple confirmatory comparisons, name the whole family and correction before accessing final data. Protected per-intent caps enter a single all-caps-must-pass utility event; report each cap without claiming a separate family of discoveries.

Before choosing `N`, use the proposed eight pilot lineages, `V_A` admission, and `V_P` planning evaluation to plan the **joint** superiority-and-coverage gate, including candidate selection, admission failures, protected failures, between-lineage variance, and author/scenario dependence. Main `A` and `E` remain inaccessible. The main seeds are disjoint from pilot seeds and fixed before main training. Treat pilot uncertainty seriously: report justified variance upper-bound or conservative sensitivity assumptions, inflated-variability and clustered-data alternatives, and nonzero variance floors when all pilot outcomes agree. Eight pilot runs cannot establish zero future variance.

Choose the smallest roster in the owner-approved finite planning set that meets the justified joint-power target and confidence-width targets for target gain and protected harm under the declared design alternative and conservative assumptions. The illustrative `32, 48, 64` roster is available for planning, but no member is currently approved or established as adequate. The study owner must supply and justify the power target, confidence widths, and alternative before calculating a favorable sample size; an alternative must exceed `delta_comp` and specify the useful-coverage distribution as well as mean gain. A normal paired-mean approximation can check arithmetic but does not replace joint finite-sample planning. The actual planning procedure, assumptions, thresholds, funded resource ceiling, required request/group counts, and resulting `N` must be frozen.

If no feasible `N` or data budget passes, the confirmatory study is a no-go. A resource-capped estimation study may be separately declared before final collection/evaluation with the same all-assigned accounting and explicit inconclusive outcome. There is no optional stopping, seed replacement, posthoc increase in evaluation size, or promotion of pilot evidence into confirmation. The historical arithmetic minimum of 13 perfect independent units for a particular binomial coverage bound is not a sample-size justification for this different, paired, clustered design.

## 8. Complete lifecycle cost and fair resource comparison

The primary resource object is a vector: nonoverlapping elapsed-work seconds, wall-clock critical-path duration, CPU and accelerator time where available, peak host/device memory, measured energy, persistent and transferred bytes, actual provider usage (zero calls are planned), and measured human effort. Memory peaks are maxima, not additive totals. Missing counters remain unknown. Currency requires identified rates, geography/time, billing rules, and date; no assumed price or business ROI is supplied here.

Publish two views, with the same all-assigned numerator and denominator:

- **System lifecycle cost:** acquiring and preparing the data and model, building and selecting all attempted changes, deploying and serving policies, and measured recovery/change operations. Show shared stages once and any allocation rule explicitly.
- **Incremental change cost given an existing parent:** all additional data/labels, encoding or cache refresh, proposal/search, selection, admission, realization, packaging, and deployment needed for a change. Parent creation is excluded from this conditional view but remains visible in the system view.

Research-only evaluation and independent replay are separate reported stages. They are not silently charged as routine production validation or omitted from total study cost. General method/reader preparation would belong to shared or arm-specific preparation if a later scope adds it; the present study has no such lane.

| Acquisition and rights | Human writing/annotation/adjudication/audit hours, payments if known, downloads/transfers, exclusion and deduplication work, rights/provenance preparation |
| Shared parent preparation | Train/development assignment, vocabulary and statistics, raw-input preprocessing, all parent epochs/checkpoint evaluations, feature generation, storage and cache construction |
| Arm preparation and every option | Context memory/index, all fitting endpoints/checkpoints and failed options, actual example presentations, selection access, structural checks, exact diagnostics, native realization and behavioral admission |
| Packaging and loading | Export, serving versus audit bytes, copy/transfer, hash/verify, imports, process start, load/deserialization, input preparation, first usable output; each nested operation belongs to exactly one aggregate stage |
| Warm service | Raw text through tokenization/features, encoder, readout, overlay/retrieval, queue policy and response materialization at batch sizes 1 and 32; cached-head timings remain a separate diagnostic |
| Change and recovery | Cache invalidation, failed-work detection/recovery, measured rollback/redeployment, restart and restore; retain failed attempts and consumed work |
| Study-only work | Final scoring, diagnostic inference, independent replay, publication/audit packaging; separately identifiable from deployment requirements |

Do not add nested verification/import timers to their containing stages or add concurrent work times to pretend they are elapsed wall time. Report both the sum of nonoverlapping work and the observed critical path. A rejected edit incurs its proposal costs while serving the parent. Shared acquisition/encoding costs may be reused, but the cache build and refresh costs cannot disappear. Report actual rollback latency when exercised separately from an unmeasured probability of needing rollback in operation.

Use a pinned hardware/OS/library/thread configuration and an isolated performance session. Randomize method order within declared hardware/lineage/case/batch blocks and rotate order across repeats. An independent engineering pilot must justify and freeze the number of fresh processes, warmup calls, measured calls, timer granularity, and permitted system load; these operational inputs remain unfilled. Retain individual measurements. Include the whole selected lineage/case/arm roster, including fallback deployments, or freeze a balanced cost subset before any method outcomes and limit cost claims to it. Do not pick only successful edits or the fastest parent.

Record CPU load/isolation, thread count, power mode, batch/input lengths, cache state, timer resolution, process failures, measurement order, and all missing readings. A fresh process with warm OS/filesystem caches is **application-cold**, not a controlled cold-disk measurement. A true cold-cache claim requires its own reproducible declared conditions. Measure process peak memory and, if supported, host/device energy over a defined boundary with instrumentation, sampling cadence, baseline/idle accounting, and coverage recorded. If energy or memory cannot be measured, retain those fields as unknown and keep any completeness criterion requiring them partial.

Within-block timing repeats estimate engineering noise; they do not increase the scientific lineage count. Report raw units, per-process medians/tails, paired differences, and uncertainty. Distinguish milliseconds per batch from per request and from throughput. One CPU, width-64 model, and batch profile cannot establish accelerator or large-model scaling. Broader device/scale coverage is a separate funded lane or an explicit limit on the cost criterion.

For a scalar additive resource measured consistently, a transparent scenario is:

`L_m(N, J, R, K) = S + P_m + sum_{j=1..J} A_m,j + N * s_m + R * r_m + K * k_m`

Here `S` is shared acquisition/parent preparation; `P_m` is method-specific reusable preparation; `A_m,j` is the complete all-attempt adaptation/deployment cost of change `j`; `s_m` is steady-state raw-input serving resource per request under the declared load; `r_m` is measured recovery cost; and `k_m` is measured restart/load cost. `N`, `J`, `R`, and `K` are explicit scenario quantities, not inferred production facts. Avoid double counting packaging/loading if it already belongs to `A`. Report time, energy, bytes, and money separately; this equation does not apply by summing peak memory.

For one change and equal declared recovery/restart assumptions, a serving crossover against baseline `b` can be written `N_star = (P_m + A_m - P_b - A_b) / (s_b - s_m)`. A positive crossover interpretation requires a positive denominator and the appropriate numerator sign; equal slopes yield no finite crossing, and the opposite signs can mean dominance or no eventual advantage. Use measured-term uncertainty and sensitivity to deployment volumes, refresh frequency, incident frequency and rates. If necessary terms are missing, the crossover is unidentified, not zero or “free.”

Cost comparisons must show useful quality, protected harm, and all-assigned coverage alongside resource usage. The default secondary display is a prespecified quality–harm–cost frontier with identical input/load assumptions. A low-cost method outside the useful region is not a cheap successful repair. A claim of quality-equivalent cost superiority would need its own frozen equivalence margins, resource endpoint, practical cost margin, and multiplicity treatment; it cannot be substituted posthoc for a failed primary gain hypothesis. This draft supplies no measured lifecycle total, savings percentage, or operational ROI.
